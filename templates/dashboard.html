AltIndexer Calculation Logic

How AltIndexer measures the altcoin market regime

AltIndexer is a forward-looking market-regime index calculated from the current signal-zone structure of supported altcoins. Instead of relying only on past 90-day price performance, it evaluates how many assets are currently positioned in model-defined buy, sell, or neutral zones.

1
Daily signal-zone scan
Each day, supported altcoins are classified as BUY, HOLD/neutral, or SELL. Their combined balance produces the daily score, which is then interpreted through the five AltIndexer regimes.
2
Buy, neutral, and sell balance
The index reflects the balance among altcoins showing positive, neutral, and defensive signal conditions.
3
Five-zone market-regime score
The final AltIndexer value is displayed as an integer from 0 to 100 across five regimes: 0–20 Strong Sell, 20–40 Sell, 40–60 Neutral, 60–80 Buy, and 80–100 Strong Buy.
4
Forward-looking interpretation
Because the calculation is based on current signal-zone conditions, AltIndexer is designed to describe the present market setup rather than only summarize past performance.
