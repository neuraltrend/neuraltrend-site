Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 07, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how NeuralTrend LLC ("NeuralTrend," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you visit https://neuraltrend.org, create an account, use our services, subscribe to NeuralTrend Pro, or contact us.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use NeuralTrend. If you have questions, contact us at contact@neuraltrend.org.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide to us

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Email address;
  • Account login and authentication information;
  • Subscription status and account plan information;
  • Messages or support requests you send to us;
  • Billing-related information processed through Stripe if you subscribe to NeuralTrend Pro.

Information collected automatically

When you use NeuralTrend, we may automatically collect technical and usage information, including:

  • IP address;
  • Browser type and device information;
  • Operating system;
  • Pages visited and features used;
  • Date and time of access;
  • Approximate location inferred from IP address;
  • Log and diagnostic information used for security, debugging, and service operation.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, sexual orientation, precise GPS location, biometric data, or health information.

2. Payment Information

NeuralTrend Pro subscriptions are processed through Stripe. When you subscribe, your payment details are entered through Stripe Checkout or Stripe billing tools. NeuralTrend does not store full credit card numbers or card security codes on its own servers.

Stripe may process payment information, billing information, and related transaction data according to its own privacy practices. You can review Stripe’s privacy information at https://stripe.com/privacy.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Create, verify, and manage user accounts;
  • Provide Free and Pro access to NeuralTrend features;
  • Process subscriptions and billing through Stripe;
  • Send account, verification, billing, security, and service-related communications;
  • Operate, maintain, debug, and improve NeuralTrend;
  • Monitor usage limits and subscription access;
  • Protect against fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and security incidents;
  • Respond to questions, support requests, and legal requests;
  • Comply with applicable legal, accounting, tax, and regulatory obligations.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

NeuralTrend may use cookies and similar technologies to keep users signed in, maintain secure sessions, remember preferences, understand site usage, diagnose issues, and improve the service.

We may use analytics tools, such as Google Analytics, to understand how users interact with the site. You can learn more about Google’s privacy practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect login, account access, and some NeuralTrend features.

5. When We Share Information

We may share information with third-party service providers that help us operate NeuralTrend. These may include:

  • Payment processors, such as Stripe;
  • Hosting and database providers;
  • Email delivery providers;
  • Analytics and performance monitoring tools;
  • Security, fraud prevention, and debugging tools;
  • Professional advisers, such as legal, tax, or accounting providers, if needed.

We may also share information if required by law, legal process, government request, enforcement of our terms, protection of our rights, prevention of fraud or abuse, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of business assets.

We do not sell personal information as a core part of our business.

6. Third-Party Websites and Services

NeuralTrend may link to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy, security, or content practices of third-party websites or services. You should review their policies before providing information to them.

7. How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain your account, process subscriptions, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, enforce our terms, and maintain business records.

If you request deletion of your account, we will delete or deactivate account information where reasonably possible. We may retain limited information where needed for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, security, or dispute-resolution purposes.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information. However, no website, database, or internet transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of certain personal information. You may also have the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise privacy rights, contact us at contact@neuraltrend.org. We may need to verify your identity before responding to your request.

10. United States Privacy Rights

Depending on your U.S. state of residence, you may have rights regarding the personal information we maintain about you, including the right to know, access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of certain personal information, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising privacy rights.

In the past twelve months, we may have collected the following categories of personal information:

  • Identifiers: such as email address, account identifiers, IP address, and Stripe customer identifiers.
  • Commercial information: such as subscription status, billing history, and transaction-related information processed through Stripe.
  • Internet or network activity: such as log data, page visits, feature usage, and interactions with the website.
  • Approximate geolocation: such as approximate location inferred from IP address.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information, biometric information, education records, or protected classification characteristics for NeuralTrend account operation.

11. International Users

If you access NeuralTrend from outside the United States, your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate. These countries may have data protection laws different from those in your location.

12. Children’s Privacy

NeuralTrend is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

13. Do-Not-Track Signals

Some browsers provide “Do Not Track” signals. Because there is not a uniform industry standard for responding to these signals, we do not currently respond to Do-Not-Track browser signals.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
