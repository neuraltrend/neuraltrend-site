Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 07, 2025

1. Subscription Billing

NeuralTrend Pro is offered as a recurring monthly or annual subscription. Payments are processed through Stripe. The subscription price and billing interval are shown before checkout.

2. Cancellation

You may cancel your NeuralTrend Pro subscription through the Stripe billing portal. After cancellation, your Pro access will generally remain available until the end of the current paid billing period. After the billing period ends, your account will return to Free access unless you resubscribe.

3. Refunds

Subscription payments are generally non-refundable once a billing period has started. However, we may review refund requests case by case, including duplicate charges, technical billing errors, or situations where required by applicable law.

4. No Refunds for Trading Outcomes

NeuralTrend does not provide refunds based on trading losses, investment outcomes, market movements, signal performance, backtest results, or dissatisfaction with hypothetical simulation results.

5. Failed or Interrupted Service

If a material technical issue prevents paid users from accessing Pro features for a significant period, we may, at our discretion, provide a partial refund, account credit, or subscription extension.

6. How to Request Help

For billing questions, cancellation issues, or refund requests, contact us at contact@neuraltrend.org using the email address associated with your NeuralTrend account.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund & Cancellation Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
