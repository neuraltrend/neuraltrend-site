Manual signal-alert dispatch

Review the current published CSV snapshots, then release eligible signal-change emails only after you are satisfied that the data are clean. Nothing on this page runs automatically.

Dispatch safeguards
  • Only opted-in, eligible users are considered.
  • Repeated BUY, HOLD, or SELL states do not email.
  • Delayed rows become one catch-up email.
  • Same-date signal changes are labeled revisions.
  • Previously sent events remain deduplicated.
  • The web button sends at most 25 emails per batch.
