Pre-launch protection
  • Sending alerts never publishes performance.
  • Sandbox publications are private and resettable.
  • Sandbox data never enters the public record.
  • Public publication stays locked until explicitly enabled.
  • Repeated signals do not generate repeated emails.
  • Working CSV changes stay private until approved.
  • Previously approved rows cannot be silently rewritten.
  • Each public asset starts only when explicitly enrolled.
  • Retired records remain available until their announced removal date.
