Important context before interpreting performance

NeuralTrend’s historical charts are model simulations, not brokerage statements and not a permanently untouched live track record. The available history includes periods used for model training, validation, testing, and later production inference. A dedicated immutable live-since-launch record is planned and will be reported separately.

Model development

Data and chronological evaluation

Each asset is processed independently using the same general feature and evaluation framework.

01

Market data

The independent market inputs are daily open, high, low, close, and volume data obtained through yfinance, generally from the beginning of available history through the latest available completed day.

Approximately 50 technical-analysis features are constructed from OHLCV. Lower-weight calendar features, such as day of week and month of year, are also included.

02

Separate asset models

EpochSignaler currently uses a separate transformer-based model for each asset, combined with hyperparameter optimization. The system does not currently use one shared universal model across all assets.

Shared-model infrastructure for related asset classes is planned, particularly to address limited history for newer assets.

03

Fixed chronological split

For model development, each asset’s observations are ordered by time and divided into the first 60% for training, the next 20% for validation, and the final 20% for testing. Future information is not randomly mixed into earlier partitions.

The currently deployed models use a fixed holdout process rather than rolling or walk-forward testing.

Signal timing

What a daily signal knows—and what it does not know

T−1

Completed market data

A signal dated day T uses completed OHLCV information only through day T−1. It does not use day T open, high, low, close, or volume data.

T

Published signal date

The resulting BUY, HOLD, or SELL state is associated with day T. This design accommodates up to a one-day delay in upstream daily-data availability.

Close

Current simulation price assumption

Historical simulations currently apply executions using the daily closing price stored for the signal date. Live paper simulations use the latest daily CSV close available to the website; they are not currently real-time exchange executions.

Simulation assumptions

How historical strategy results are calculated

Signal actions

BUY invests according to the selected position-size rule, SELL reduces the current position according to that rule, and HOLD does not execute a trade.

Transaction costs

The current fixed assumptions are 1.00% per executed crypto side and 0.10% per executed stock side. These are modeling assumptions, not guaranteed user costs.

Open positions

An open position at the end of a selected horizon is marked to market. NeuralTrend does not invent a final SELL or charge a final exit cost without a SELL signal.

Benchmark

Buy & Hold is measured over the same period with an entry transaction cost. For whole-share stock simulations, residual uninvested cash remains in benchmark equity.

Costs not currently modeled

Slippage, bid–ask spread variation, DEX gas costs, liquidity-dependent price impact, market impact, taxes, borrow costs, and exchange-specific fee tiers are not modeled separately. Actual trading results can therefore differ materially from simulations.

Model maintenance

Retraining and model-selection policy

Current process

Model monitoring and retraining are currently manual. Assets with weaker recent strategy performance relative to Buy & Hold—typically reviewed across approximately one week to one-to-three months—receive higher retraining priority.

Depending on recent inference quality, a model may remain deployed for several weeks, several months, or longer. Automated monitoring and retraining are planned.

Why this matters

Because recent performance influences which models are retrained, aggregate historical results should not be interpreted as one model selected once and left untouched forever. Retraining can introduce model-selection effects that a fixed historical holdout does not fully capture.

NeuralTrend therefore labels these results as historical model simulations and will report a separate immutable live record when that tracking system is introduced.

History integrity

Historical signal preservation and revision policy

Append-only by default

When a newly trained model is deployed, NeuralTrend normally appends new dates, features, and signals while preserving previously published historical signal rows.

Rare revisions

A full data cleanup or correction may occasionally require historical changes. When that occurs, the affected asset, period, reason, and whether prior signals changed will be documented in the public change log below before revised results are presented.

Release audit

The project includes a signal-history audit tool that compares previously published Date/signal rows with the next release and flags unexpected historical changes for review.

Limitations

What the results do not establish

  • Historical performance does not guarantee future performance.
  • The fixed 60/20/20 split is not equivalent to continuous walk-forward validation.
  • The current website does not yet provide real-time execution or an immutable live track record.
  • Upstream providers may discontinue assets or revise source data.
  • NeuralTrend does not remove assets solely because their model performed poorly.

Use NeuralTrend as a research tool

Review current signals, assumptions, historical behavior, and paper simulations together. Do not rely on a single return figure when making a financial decision.
