How to interpret this record

The chart is a hypothetical backtest-style calculation limited to the asset's approved tracking window. BUY and SELL actions use approved daily closing prices. Current fixed transaction-cost assumptions are included; slippage, taxes, liquidity limits, and brokerage execution are not.

The working model CSV may contain a longer history. Rows before the public start date and rows not yet approved by an administrator are excluded here.
