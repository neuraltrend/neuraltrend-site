FAQ

Buy-and-hold vs AI strategy FAQ

What is buy-and-hold?

Buy-and-hold means buying an asset and holding it through the selected period without using active trading signals.

What is an AI signal strategy?

In NeuralTrend, an AI signal strategy follows BUY, HOLD, and SELL signal states generated by the EpochSignaler model.

Does AI strategy always beat buy-and-hold?

No. NeuralTrend does not guarantee outperformance. The comparison exists so users can evaluate when the AI strategy outperformed or underperformed the benchmark.

Can I backtest the strategy?

Yes. NeuralTrend includes historical backtesting tools for supported assets, with expanded access available through NeuralTrend Pro.

Is this financial advice?

No. NeuralTrend is an informational research and simulation platform. It does not provide personalized financial advice or guarantee future results.
